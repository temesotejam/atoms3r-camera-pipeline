# Foot-marker energy allocation, version 1

This experimental outer loop applies only to the 8-degree wheel-work walking mode. The 8-degree setting remains the gradual-start baseline, not a promise of either peak amplitude or straight travel. It compares forward camera-frame marker offsets from each foot's zero, nearest to the positive/left and negative/right body peaks. The observation is not a ground-frame step length or heading measurement.

After three recent samples for each side, a normalized imbalance slowly shifts the corresponding target potential energies by factors (1-bias) and (1+bias), at most 0.02 per second and within +/-0.20. The controller keeps the existing bounded wheel-work choice, energy prediction, startup stages, wheel speed feedback, and stop behavior. Low motion, missing or stale samples gradually return the bias to zero. Camera data are selected within 100 ms of the peak using recent frame history, including frames received before retrospective peak detection. Log fields foot_energy_bias, foot_energy_left_px, foot_energy_right_px, and foot_energy_valid expose the decision. Mean pixels are clipped to nonnegative forward displacement and exclude values beyond 80 px.

Replay of runs 443, 387, and 296 verified observation gating and allocation direction, not closed-loop behavior: the recorded motion cannot show what this changed controller would have done. The direction and strength of the coupling from side energy to foot motion, the relation between camera marker motion and ground travel, and heading correction remain to be validated with the new controller running on the robot. The existing two-peak MPC remains a bounded swing model, not a learned direct foot-motion predictor.
