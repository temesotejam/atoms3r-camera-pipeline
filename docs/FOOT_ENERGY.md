# Cycle-based foot observation and guarded energy identification

Version 1.0.56 removes the prior unconditional, peak-snapshot based +/-20% allocation. The usual gradual 8-degree energy controller and its bounded motor and stop paths remain in place. The new feature is limited to that 8-degree wheel-work mode. An 8-degree setting is the reference, not a guarantee of actual amplitude or gait.

The observer measures each marker's peak-to-peak X range over a complete negative-to-negative body cycle, not its zero-referenced peak or a signed ground step. The cycle needs at least five valid frames per side, no interval gap over 250 ms, and each endpoint gap at most 150 ms and 20% of the cycle, no inter-frame jump over 30 px, and a duration between 250 and 2000 ms. It uses exposure timestamps and waits for late camera delivery. Positive/negative pitch has not been independently mapped to physical left/right. Pixel ranges are camera/body-relative and may reflect projection or marker errors; they cannot directly establish foot lift, contact, stride or heading. Camera sampling limits the measured range. Logged `foot_energy_left_px` and `foot_energy_right_px` now mean the latest complete-cycle ranges, not the prior three-peak means.

Before any trial, require recent valid image cycles, nontrivial motion, a stable gradual-start stage, and all 8 recent in-support, fresh-speed, nonzero, unsaturated, non-extrapolated motor decisions, including at least three per side. Only then use small energy allocation factors 1-b and 1+b, with |b| <= 0.02 and a slew of at most 0.01 per second. The within-run exploratory sequence is -0.02,+0.02,-0.02 with at least one second settling and three complete cycles per window; the angle-energy and bounded wheel-work calculation is retained. It does not prescribe a foot response sign. On three windows, the normalized image-range difference and mean commanded charge difference must both reverse consistently, exceed thresholds, and occur within compatible voltage and per-side speed ranges. The candidate trial biases are compared with an interpolated neutral point; near-zero or ambiguous improvement returns to neutral. Otherwise the bias unwinds to zero. The comparisons share a middle period and do not prove causality. Successful recognition remains provisional; apparent repeated worsening, a stage change, stale images, or any unsupported command aborts and unwinds the bias. No repeated probing occurs in that run. `foot_energy_phase`: 0 waiting, 1/2/3 trial periods, 4 provisional response, 5 fallback; `foot_energy_valid` reports fresh consecutive image cycles, not causal validation. `foot_energy_reason` is 0 none, 1 stale/invalid image, 2 changed stage, 3 unsupported command, 4 inconclusive response, 5 apparent worsening.

The actual latest run 319 had frequent positive-side saturation/extrapolation and negative-side support failures. Therefore its recorded data would not qualify for this trial: the controller must remain at neutral rather than forcing a larger correction or claiming feet are aligned. The current two-peak MPC does not predict foot movement. Run data, physical yaw, toe contact, control latency and behavior with this firmware remain to be measured on hardware.
