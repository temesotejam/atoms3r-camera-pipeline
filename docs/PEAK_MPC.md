# Experimental two-peak controller

For target 8 degrees, a zero-crossing decision is evaluated over two future peaks. The first move is the existing energy solver's choice or an alternate produced by the same solver with a cap at most 5 ms shorter. Positive 400 mA and walking-branch negative 300 mA only; all existing limits and STOP handling remain in force. The second move is hypothetical and is recalculated at the next crossing. Predictions are not supported on every crossing; fallback uses the existing solver.

The side-specific linear transition models use rate, signed pre-input speed, previous peak, width and voltage. Each predicts the next peak, following zero-cross rate, and following pre-input speed. They were fitted to nine walking recordings (run IDs 27579224, 275876204, 29616798, 30059236, 31767392, 33892778, 35471660, 50202353, 55836070), with 5–24 s transitions. These are observations generated by previous controllers; alternative pulse-width effects are confounded and are not independently identified. Limits in `peak_mpc.h` are marginal domains, not proof that all combinations are sampled.

The score contains squared target-angle errors and a penalty on the average of two phase-specific crossing speeds. This speed term is a proxy, not a time-averaged wheel momentum or a yaw measurement. The first-move deviation is limited to 5 ms. Missing/stale speed, invalid model outputs, unsupported combinations, or inability to evaluate either choice returns to the existing control. No closed-loop hardware validation or guarantee of ±8 degrees, symmetric spin, or straight walking is claimed.

The zero-cross event records `mpc_valid`, `mpc_alternate`, `mpc_second_peak_deg`, and `mpc_speed_proxy_rpm`. `tools/peak_mpc_model_check.py` reproduces leave-one-run-out two-peak replay, using *observed* future actions; its errors are not counterfactual or closed-loop accuracy. In the latest run 29616798 the 45 historical-action replay samples gave 0.47 degree and 20.5 rpm mean absolute error for the second peak and following crossing speed, with maxima 1.09 degrees and 80.6 rpm. Differences in voltage, foot motion, and conditions limit comparability.
